# Add your photos here

The site already references the following image files. Put your photos in this folder using these exact names (JPG, PNG, WebP, or change the extensions in `app/page.tsx`):

- `hero.jpg` — your favorite portrait / moment from the day
- `chapter-01.jpg` — first place
- `chapter-02.jpg` — walk / activity
- `chapter-03.jpg` — dinner / final stop
- `food-feature.jpg` — a close-up of the food or drinks
- `01-arrival.jpg` through `05-evening.jpg` — gallery moments

Until you add a file, the site displays a tasteful placeholder in its frame. Replace the bracketed copy in `app/page.tsx` with your details and names.
